**Status: Closed (October 2026).** LensDigest has been shut down, and lensdigest.com is no longer available. This entry remains as a record of the project.

LensDigest was the editorial site in the lab: a photography and videography digest built around frequent publishing, clean search intent, and fast reading.

The project explored the publishing workflow around the site: turning a broad camera niche into consistently packaged pages that were useful to readers and legible to search engines.

## What It Explored

- High-frequency publishing with consistent editorial structure.
- Clear page structure for camera, video, creator, and gear topics.
- A lightweight static delivery model for daily updates.
