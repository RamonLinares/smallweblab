**Status: Closed (October 2026).** Arcana Muse has been shut down, and arcanamuse.com is no longer available. This entry remains as a record of the project.

Arcana Muse was an AI tarot-guidance experiment built around reflection, ritual, and follow-up context.

The design question was how to make an AI interaction feel specific without pretending to predict the future. The product explored interpretation, practical framing, and repeat visits.

## What It Explored

- A consumer AI interface with a strong theme and clear boundaries.
- Prompting that supported reflection rather than generic horoscope copy.
- A product surface where visual mood, memory, and advice needed to fit together.
