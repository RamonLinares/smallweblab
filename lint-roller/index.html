Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email cameragearweb@gmail.com and I'll get back to you.

Something was flagged that shouldn't be? Long-press it and choose Never Flag This.

Deleted something by mistake? Deleted items stay in the Photos app under Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Some photos weren't compared? Photos stored only in iCloud are skipped by default because downloading them is slow. Turn on Compare iCloud Photos in the ⋯ menu.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Lint Roller does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • All analysis of your photos and screenshots happens on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.
  • The app does not connect to the internet, and has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • To avoid analysing the same photos twice, the app keeps its own analysis results (such as the text it read from a screenshot) in its private storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Photos are only deleted or hidden when you confirm it in the system prompt.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: cameragearweb@gmail.com.
